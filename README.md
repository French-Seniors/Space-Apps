# NASA Space Apps Challenge

We are Arthur and Antoine, two French high school seniors based in Singapore. We are participating in the NASA Space Apps Challenge to test ourselves and build hands-on experience.

## Space Sweeper

Space Sweeper is an interactive resource-management game developed to raise awareness about the growing, critical threat of orbital debris. Addressing the challenge of simulating a complete space mission, our game puts players in charge of a vital clean-up operation in Earth's orbit.

Players alternate between a planning phase—making engineering trade-offs to upgrade energy production, fuel capacity, and magnet power using limited budgets—and an active mission phase where they navigate a spacecraft to collect debris before their fuel runs out. By balancing these competing demands, players experience the complex realities of mission design.

Ultimately, Space Sweeper makes the abstract problem of space pollution tangible, educating players on why keeping our orbit clean is essential for the safety of astronauts, satellites, and future space exploration.
